COHERENCE is installed

  1. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins. In Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan. COHERENCE is listed under Phase Systems.
  2. With an SSL UC1 or UF1: open SSL 360, then rescan in 360 Link.
