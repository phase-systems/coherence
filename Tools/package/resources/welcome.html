COHERENCE @VERSION@

A channel strip plug-in by Phase Systems: a four-band EQ with independent Mid/Side settings, a compressor, a Mid/Side level stage, and input and output with metering. AU and VST3, for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 11 or later.

Please quit your DAW and SSL 360 before installing.
